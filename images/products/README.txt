Put your PRODUCT PHOTOS in this folder.

Tips for good photos:
- Take them in soft, natural light (near a window).
- Use a plain cream or neutral background.
- Square (1:1) photos look best in the website grid.

After adding a photo here, open ../../content.js and set the product's
"image" to:   "images/products/your-file-name.jpg"

Example:
   image: "images/products/blue-pouch.jpg",

File names: use simple lowercase names with no spaces
(good: blue-pouch.jpg   avoid: Blue Pouch (1).JPG)
