# Jiya Handmade Creations — Brand Guidelines

_Version 1.0 · Guntur, India_

> **Handmade with love, made just for you.**

These guidelines keep everything Jiya makes and shares — packaging, posts, the
website, and messages to customers — feeling like one warm, consistent brand.

---

## 1. Brand Story

Jiya Handmade Creations is a small-batch, hand-knitted and crocheted woollen
brand based in Guntur. Every piece is made by hand, in limited quantities, with
care poured into each stitch. The brand made its market debut for Rakhi — selling
out of crochet rakhis two days ahead of the festival, both at a Guntur stall and
through Instagram orders.

Jiya is about the little handmade things that make everyday moments feel special:
a pouch for your phone, a charm for your bag, a cap to keep a loved one warm, a
rakhi tied with real affection.

### What we make
- **Crochet items** (festival & occasion pieces, e.g. Rakhi)
- **Woollen mobile pouches**
- **Hangings / charms** for mobiles and handbags
- **Hand-knitted woollen caps** (baby, kids & adults)

## 2. Mission & Values

**Values**

- **Small batch, high quality.** We'd rather sell out than cut corners.

## 3. Voice & Tone

Jiya speaks like a warm, friendly maker — never corporate.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Warm, personal, sincere | Salesy, pushy, hype-y |
| Simple, everyday language | Jargon or complicated words |
| Grateful & community-minded | Cold or transactional |
| Gently playful | Sarcastic or ironic |

**Signature phrases**
- "Handmade with love, made just for you."
- "Thank you" (always — it's on every card and every order)
- "Made just for you"

**Example — good:** _"Thank you so much for your order! Your pouch is being
hand-made with love and will be ready in 3–4 days. 💛"_

## 4. Logo

The primary logo is the **`jiya` script wordmark** with **"Handmade Creations"**
set beneath it, enclosed by a delicate botanical wreath with a small heart and a
ball of yarn.

**Usage rules**
- Keep clear space around the logo — at least the height of the letter "j".
- Never stretch, squash, rotate, or recolor the wordmark.
- Preferred on cream or white backgrounds. On busy photos, use a soft
  cream/white panel behind it.
- Don't add drop shadows, outlines, or effects.
- Minimum size: the word "jiya" should never be smaller than ~24px wide on
  screen so it stays legible.

**Logo variations**
- **Full logo:** wordmark + "Handmade Creations" + wreath (primary use).
- **Wordmark only:** `jiya` for small spaces (favicons, watermarks).

## 5. Colour Palette

Drawn directly from the brand card — soft, natural, and warm.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary accent | Terracotta | `#C97C5D` | Buttons, links, highlights, hearts |
| Secondary | Blush Coral | `#E0A085` | Soft accents, hovers, tags |
| Nature | Sage Green | `#9CAA85` | Leaves, botanical accents, secondary tags |
| Text | Cocoa Brown | `#4A3F35` | Headings & body text |
| Muted text | Taupe | `#7A6A5A` | Captions, secondary text |
| Background | Warm Cream | `#FBF7F0` | Page background |
| Surface | Soft Ivory | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, panels |
| Border | Sand | `#EAE0D2` | Dividers, card borders |

**Rules**
- Cream/ivory should dominate — keep it light and airy.
- Terracotta is the hero accent; use it for anything clickable.
- Sage is a supporting colour — great for botanical touches, not for text.
- Keep enough contrast: dark cocoa text on cream, never pale text on pale.

## 6. Typography

The card pairs an elegant script with a clean, friendly sans-serif. The website
mirrors this using free Google Fonts.

| Use | Font | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logo / script accents | **Dancing Script** | The `jiya` feel — headings & flourishes only, never body text |
| Headings | **Cormorant Garamond** | Elegant serif for section titles |
| Body & UI | **Poppins** / **Nunito Sans** | Clean, rounded, friendly — all paragraphs, buttons, labels |
| Small caps taglines | **Poppins**, letter-spaced, UPPERCASE | e.g. "HANDMADE WITH LOVE" |

**Rules**
- Never use the script font for long text — it's for short, special moments.
- Keep body text at a comfortable size (16px+), generous line spacing.
- Uppercase taglines get wide letter-spacing (like the card), used sparingly.

## 7. Photography & Imagery

- **Natural light**, soft and warm — shoot near a window.
- **Cream / neutral backgrounds** (a plain cloth, wood, or the brand card).
- Style products with a few **botanical props** (dried flowers, sprigs) to echo
  the card's wreath.
- Show **scale and use** — a pouch holding a phone, a charm on a bag, a cap being
  worn. Lifestyle shots sell handmade.
- Keep editing light and true-to-colour — woollens should look cosy, not
  over-saturated.
- Square (1:1) or portrait (4:5) works best for Instagram and the website grid.

## 9. Quick Do's & Don'ts

**Do**
- Keep everything warm, handmade, and personal.
- Lead with cream, accent with terracotta.
- Say thank you — every single time.
- Show products in real life and at real scale.

**Don't**
- Recolour or distort the logo.
- Use the script font for paragraphs.
- Over-saturate or heavily filter product photos.
- Sound corporate or pushy.
